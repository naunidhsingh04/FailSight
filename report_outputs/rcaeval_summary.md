# RCAEval external test (testing only)

Nothing was trained or tuned on this data. The two unsupervised per-app detectors of the testbed (robust z-score and IsolationForest, each learned from the first 150 s of the case itself) were applied with their alert thresholds FROZEN from the testbed train runs (robust z-score 10, IsolationForest 0.5672) and the frozen alert rule (2 of the last 3 windows). Generated by `python scripts/rcaeval_eval.py`.

## Data

RCAEval RE1 (metrics only), Zenodo records 14504480 / 14504481: RE1-OB (Online Boutique), RE1-SS (Sock Shop), RE1-TT (Train Ticket), 390 MB downloaded in total. 150 cases: for each system and fault type (cpu, mem, disk, delay, loss) the runs 1 and 2 of the five services = 10 cases per cell. Every case has an explicit injection time (`inject_time.txt`), so no assumption about a normal period was needed. Normal data before the injection: Online Boutique 32 min, Sock Shop 4 min, Train Ticket 6 min after the 150 s learning period; windows are 30 s with a 10 s step.

## Features available (generic aggregates present in every selected case of a system; `rcaeval_feature_mapping.csv`)

- Online Boutique: used cpu_total, mem_total, error_rate; present in only some cases (not used): latency_p95, request_rate; not present: net_rx, net_tx, disk_read, disk_write
- Sock Shop: used cpu_total, mem_total, net_rx, net_tx, disk_read, disk_write, latency_p95, request_rate, error_rate; not present: none
- Train Ticket: used cpu_total, mem_total, net_rx, net_tx, disk_read, disk_write, latency_p95, request_rate, error_rate; not present: none

## Reading the numbers

- Robust z-score: detects 20% to 36% of the faults per system (placebo 0% to 10%) with 0.0 to 2.8 false alarms per hour in the normal period.
- IsolationForest: detects 68% to 86% but also alerts at the placebo times in 31% to 79% of the cases and raises 10.6 to 17.2 false alarms per hour, so most of its detections are not specific to the fault.
- Cells have 10 cases each; false-alarm rates in Sock Shop and Train Ticket come from only a few minutes of normal data per case and are coarse. Delays are on a 10 s grid.
- The testbed's alert thresholds were chosen on 14 testbed-specific signals; here the signals are different aggregates, so this is a test of how far the same unsupervised recipe carries, not a fair benchmark of root-cause analysis.

## Supervised testbed models

Not applied. The frozen random forest takes 78 inputs (39 testbed features and their baseline-relative forms: per-request statistics, log counts, thread counts, event-loop lag, database-pool wait, in-flight requests). RCAEval has at most 9 generic aggregates per system (CPU, memory, request rate, latency, errors, network and disk where present) with different meaning and units, and none of the log, thread, loop-lag or pool signals. Mapping them onto the model's inputs would mean inventing equivalences, so no supervised result is reported; retraining on this data was not done because nothing may be tuned on external data.

## Limits

Public benchmark, 150 cases from three systems, RE1 metrics only (no traces or logs), unsupervised detectors with frozen thresholds from another application: a first look at transfer, not evidence that FailSight works on other systems.
